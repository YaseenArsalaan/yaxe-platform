# 🤖 HAYS – AI Business Assistant

HAYS is the intelligent AI assistant integrated into the YAXΞ platform.

It helps businesses provide fast, accurate, and conversational support by understanding customer questions and delivering context-aware responses.

## ✨ Key Capabilities

- 💬 AI-powered customer conversations
- 📚 Answers business and product questions
- 🛍️ Assists customers before making purchases
- 📄 Retrieves information from business knowledge
- ⚡ Provides intelligent real-time assistance
- 🤖 Natural language conversations
